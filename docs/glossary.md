# Glossary

- **RAG:** Retrieval-Augmented Generation; retrieve evidence and provide it to a generator.
- **Chunk:** A retrievable segment of a source document.
- **Embedding:** Numeric representation of content in a vector space.
- **Vector database:** Storage/search system for embedding vectors.
- **Reranker:** Model or algorithm that reorders retrieved candidates.
- **Grounding:** Constraining claims to supplied evidence.
- **Abstention:** Declining to answer when evidence is insufficient.
- **Recall@k:** Fraction of relevant items found in the top k results.
- **MRR:** Mean Reciprocal Rank of the first relevant result.
- **Prompt injection:** Untrusted content attempting to manipulate model behavior.
- **Tenant isolation:** Ensuring one customer cannot access another customer's data.
